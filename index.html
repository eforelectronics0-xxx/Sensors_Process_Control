✎Unit wise summary

Unit I — Fundamentals of Sensors

Definition, need and types of sensors; detailed study of temperature, stress/strain, light, chemical, gas, vibration, displacement, force, torque, pressure, position, motion, level, flow, humidity, pH, soil, smoke, sound and smart sensors with diagrams, construction, working and applications; and the eleven performance parameters of a sensor.

  • Introduction to Sensors
  • Temperature Sensors
  • Stress, Strain and Load Cells
  • Light Sensors
  • Chemical Sensors
  • Gas Sensors
  • Vibration Sensors
  • Displacement and Force Sensors
  • Torque and Pressure Sensors
  • Position and Motion Sensors
  • Level and Flow Sensors
  • Humidity and pH Sensors
  • Soil and Smoke Sensors
  • Sound Sensors
  • Smart Sensors
  • Specifications & Performance Parameters
Unit II — Actuators

Definition and principle of actuators, difference between sensor, transducer and actuator; pressure and flow control actuators (valves); power control devices SCR, TRIAC and MOSFET; magnetic control devices relay and solenoid; electromechanical motors — servo, DC, AC and stepper.

  • Introduction to Actuators
  • Pressure & Flow Control Actuators
  • Power Control Devices
  • Magnetic Control Devices
  • Electromechanical Motors
Unit III — Fundamentals of Process Automation

Process control system and its elements; continuous, discrete state and composite control; process characteristics — process equation, load, lag and self regulation; control system parameters — error, variable range, control parameter range, control time and cycling.

  • Process Control Systems
  • Process Characteristics
  • Control System Parameters
Unit IV — Controller Modes

Discontinuous modes — two position and multi position; continuous modes — proportional, integral and derivative; composite modes — PI, PD and PID with tuning and response comparison.

  • Discontinuous Controller Modes
  • Continuous Controller Modes
  • Composite Controller Modes
