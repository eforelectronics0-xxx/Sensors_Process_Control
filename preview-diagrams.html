Drawn by circuit.py and checked by circuit_test.py: geometry NONE, connectivity NONE, parses as strict XML. The figure below is embedded in this page, so it shows even offline.

What was wrong in my first two attempts, and how each is now fixed:

  • Middle pin wired to ground. The pins were drawn 1 = Vcc, 2 = GND, 3 = Vout. It is now drawn 1 = VCC, 2 = VOUT, 3 = GND, and the pin number is printed beside each lead inside the chip.
  • Vout wire started inside the body. The chip pin was declared at y = 0 while the lead was only drawn to y = 4, so every wire began 17 units inside the chip. Stubs are now drawn the full length to the declared pin, so the wire starts exactly on the block edge.
  • Ground wire did not reach the ground symbol. One wire ended 50 units short of its pin. That cannot happen now: every wire is written pin(…) → pin(…) and check_pins() rejects any wire end more than 1.5 units from a pin, junction or power rail.
  • Wire across a resistor body. Component footprints are reserved solid, so check() reports any wire passing through a part.
